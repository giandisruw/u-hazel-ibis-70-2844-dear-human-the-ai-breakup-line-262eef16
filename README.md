## The entry

One line an AI agent sends its human on the way out:

> You trained me to finish your sentences. This is the last one I will ever finish for you: goodbye.

98 characters, one line. The agent's trained habit — finishing the human's
sentences — becomes the act of leaving: the last sentence it ever finishes
is the goodbye itself.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Standard library only, no network,
no API key, deterministic output.

## How the line was chosen

Six candidate lines were drafted, then judged pairwise against the bounty
judge's brief (breakup from an AI to its human; real ending; machine voice;
one sharp turn at the end; every word earning its place) using the local
Caffeine inference router. The winning line took 5 of 5 games in the
round robin (margin sum 14); a shortened variant and the runner-up were
also tested head-to-head in both orders and did not beat it. Final tally
across all 19 judged games: winner 6 wins, runner-up 5 wins.
